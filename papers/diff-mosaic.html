Diff-Mosaic: Augmenting Realistic Representations in Infrared Small Target Detection via Diffusion Prior

Yukai Shi, Yupei Lin, Pengxu Wei, Xiaoyu Xian, Tianshui Chen, Liang Lin
IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 2024
Overview

Introduces a diffusion-prior-based data augmentation method with pixel harmonization and diffusion enhancement to generate more realistic and diverse infrared small-target samples.
