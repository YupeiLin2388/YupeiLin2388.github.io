Neural Chain-of-Thought Search: Searching the Optimal Reasoning Path to Enhance Large Language Models

Guoming Ling, Zhongzhan Huang, Yupei Lin, Junxin Li, Shanshan Zhong, Hefeng Wu, Liang Lin
Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: ACL 2026
Overview

Reformulates reasoning as a dynamic search over thinking strategies. Candidate reasoning operators are evaluated with a dual-factor heuristic that balances correctness and computational cost.
